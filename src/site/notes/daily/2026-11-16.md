- 12:59 a thought that I had in the middle of the night, thinking about the dangers of people becoming used to 'talking' to LLM bots, specifically this is around **nuance** (uncertainty, nuance, messiness and friction). That these tools (commercial, 'frontier' ones specifically) are designed to be seamless and efficient degrades a quality extremely important in communicating with others, which is navigating and negotiating things that are undesirable, its a discomfort that can bring growth. If everything is easy and seamless we lose this ability - ie. talking to a bot is very transactional, you don't need to concern yourself with navigating grey areas or nuance and you don't have to care about the feelings of another
