Mahalia submitted a very comprehensive report and we would like to confirm this as a PhD.

Here are some recommendations for the next stages of the research:

**Positioning and theoretical framing**

·       Clarify what your position is and the value system that underpins this. Develop this further to take a clearer stance within this complex and challenging context.

·       Explore key terms in your project and their interrelationships: intelligence, learning, knowledge, research, training. Identify where they connect, intersect and differ – also depending on the positioning you take.

·       Consider whether your sections of the PhD on reflexive practice on the one hand and creative ecdication in HE can be brought together. Does this link need stronger theoretical bridging?

**Literature/Practice review**

·       Review work of Neil Postman – how is tech changing education

·       Strengthen the contextual review of education literature on academic integrity in the context of Gen AI and approaches taken by Art/Design HE institutions

·       Relate your project to current UAL developments and the wider sector.

·       Include existing examples of playful engagement with LLM bots (i.e.Bratton bots into discord)

**Audiences**

·       Identify your audiences. We discussed that there might be several, and it would help to define each one clearly. Engaging with other disciplines through your trans/inter/multi-disciplinary work could help you to scale your research and consider where your research will have impact.

·       Present at conferences (ELIA, Cumulus, ECREA for example) to test it with different audiences

**Planning and Preparation**

·       Plan your research time carefully with your line manager to protect your 20% research time.

·       Rehearse presentations to build confidence and prepare for the Viva.
